B2I Intelligence Agent

Document intelligence pipeline

An end-to-end app that finds the right contact person (SPOC) in college PDF reports and loads their name, designation, email and phone into MySQL.

  • Automated downloader scrapes college websites, uploads reports to AWS S3 and saves links to the database.
  • OCR for scanned pages, High / Medium / Low confidence scoring and automatic duplicate detection.
  • Human-in-the-loop review with validation, audit logging, a batch-import CLI with dry-run, and transactional pushes with rollback.
